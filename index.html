Architecture & Intelligence Pipeline

  • Haar Feature Classifiers: Multi-stage cascade integration (`haarcascade_frontalface`, `haarcascade_eye`, `haarcascade_mcs_nose`, `haarcascade_mcs_mouth`).
  • Image Pre-processing: Grayscale normalization, histogram equalization, and adaptive Gaussian noise filtering.
  • Bounding Geometry Tracker: Dynamic scaling factor and minNeighbors tuning to eliminate false positives in diverse lighting.
  • Dataset Synthesizer: Automated generation pipeline for synthetic image labeling and training data augmentation.
